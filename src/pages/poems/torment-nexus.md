<pre class="py-4 leading-relaxed">
Have you ever read that old sci-fi book
<i>Oh Please, Do Not Build the Torment Nexus</i>?
If not, you really should go take a look,
to find a solve for all which has vexed us.

In it there's this device that is quite cool,
that can think and speak just like you and me.
The ultimate productivity tool!
As I read its value was plain to see.

Why <i>not</i> create this miracle machine
to help us conquer all that is mundane?
Like reading and writing. Boring. Routine.
Imagine all that we could stand to gain!

So thank you for listening to my pitch;
I trust you see how much this will enrich.
</pre>
